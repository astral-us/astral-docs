# Astral Domain Detector — Training Results

> Data for paper / blog. Models trained June 12–13 2026 on hoopoe (2× RTX 5090).

---

## Why a custom detector?

The eco drone stack runs YOLOv8n pretrained on COCO-80 classes. COCO has no class for
*drone/UAV, person-from-altitude, landing pad, powerline,* or *aerial bicycle/motorcycle* —
the exact objects a drone needs to track, avoid, or land near. We trained a drop-in
replacement that speaks the same ONNX/TensorRT interface.

## Training data

### Detector v1 — sim only

| Source | Scenes | Frames | Boxes | Classes covered |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|-----------------|
| Isaac Sim (office) | 1 | 6,000 | — | vehicle (2), drone (4) |
| Isaac Sim (warehouse) | 1 | 6,000 | — | vehicle (2), drone (4) |
| Isaac Sim (hospital) | 1 | 6,000 | — | vehicle (2), drone (4) |
| **Total** | **3** | **18,000** | **~12,300** | **2 of 9 classes** |

Labels were auto-generated by projecting known vehicle poses (Nova Carter rover, Crazyflie
quadrotor) through camera intrinsics at each frame — no manual annotation.

### Detector v2 — sim + VisDrone

| Source | Frames | Boxes | Classes added |
|--------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Isaac Sim (v1 data) | 18,000 | 12,788 | vehicle (2), drone (4) |
| VisDrone2019-DET train | 6,471 | 343,205 | person_aerial (1), vehicle (2), bicycle_motorcycle (3) |
| VisDrone2019-DET val | 548 | 38,759 | same |
| **Total** | **25,019** | **394,752** | **4 of 9 classes** |

VisDrone class mapping: pedestrian + people → `person_aerial`; car + van + truck + bus +
tricycle → `vehicle`; bicycle + motor → `bicycle_motorcycle`.

### Detector v3 — v2 + drone oversampling (training in progress)

v2 revealed a class-imbalance failure: drone boxes were 3.5% of the merged dataset, causing
the model to learn to ignore them (AP50 dropped from 0.47 to 0.01).

Fix: 4× oversampling of drone-containing train images.

| Stat | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Drone images (train) | 8,982 | 35,928 |
| Total train images | 21,471 | 48,417 |
| Drone box fraction | 3.5% | ~10% |

## Results

### Detector v1 — sim-only baseline

**Validation set:** 18,000 sim frames (20% held-out split)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| mAP@0.5 | **0.471** |
| mAP@0.5:0.95 | 0.340 |
| Precision | 0.791 |
| Recall | 0.414 |
| Training time | ~21 min (1279 s) |
| ONNX latency (5090) | 18 ms / 55 FPS |

Per-class AP@0.5:

| Class | AP@0.5 | Note |
|-------|--------|------|
| vehicle | **0.895** | Strong — rovers and vehicles visible in all 3 scenes |
| drone | 0.047 | Weak — drone is small, visually similar to background |
| person_aerial | 0.000 | No training data |
| bicycle_motorcycle | 0.000 | No training data |

Confusion matrix observation: vehicle predicted correctly 92% of the time; 99% of true
`drone` detections were missed (predicted background) — confirming drone AP near zero.

### Detector v2 — sim + VisDrone

**Validation set:** 3,248 VisDrone val images (official split) + sim val frames

| Metric | Value | Δ vs v1 |
|--------|-------|---------|
| mAP@0.5 | **0.376** | −0.095 (overall lower — drone regression) |
| mAP@0.5:0.95 | 0.193 | |
| Precision | 0.724 | |
| Recall | 0.352 | |
| Training time | ~32 min (1904 s) | +11 min (larger dataset) |

Per-class AP@0.5:

| Class | v1 AP@0.5 | v2 AP@0.5 | Change |
|-------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| person_aerial | 0.000 | **0.377** | +0.377 ✅ |
| vehicle | 0.895 | **0.755** | −0.140 (harder real-world val) |
| bicycle_motorcycle | 0.000 | **0.365** | +0.365 ✅ |
| drone | 0.047 | **0.010** | −0.037 ❌ (class imbalance) |

**Root cause of drone regression:** VisDrone is shot *from* drones, not *at* drones — zero
drone-as-target annotations in that dataset. After merging, drone boxes shrank from ~50% to
3.5% of all training boxes. The model learned to suppress drone predictions entirely.

v2 confusion matrix: vehicle TP rate 73%; person_aerial TP rate 36% (hard — small,
oblique, occluded); bicycle_motorcycle TP rate 30%; drone TP rate ~0%.

### v2 qualitative results (VisDrone val)

Model correctly fires on dense urban aerial scenes:
- Up to 76 detections per frame (busy intersection)
- vehicle confidence 0.75–0.91 in crowded traffic
- person_aerial confidence 0.25–0.67 (lower confidence = small objects, expected)
- bicycle_motorcycle correctly separated from vehicles at 0.27–0.74

### v1 qualitative results (Isaac Sim hospital val)

Model fires on the Nova Carter rover in sim hospital scenes at 0.4–0.9 confidence. No
false positives on empty corridors. One false positive: wheelchair detected as `vehicle 0.46`
(reasonable — wheelchair is a wheeled ground vehicle).

## Key observations for paper/blog

1. **Sim-to-real gap is asymmetric by class.** Vehicle transfers well (sim 0.895 → real 0.755).
   Drone does not transfer at all because sim drones look nothing like real drones in a
   real-world context, and VisDrone provides zero drone-as-target examples.

2. **Class imbalance is the dominant failure mode at this scale.** Merging a 343K-box real
   dataset with a 12K-box sim dataset destroyed the minority class. A 4× oversample (v3)
   is expected to recover it.

3. **VisDrone is an effective free label source for aerial person/vehicle.** Zero manual
   annotation — just a class-mapping table from their 10 classes to ours. person_aerial went
   from 0 → 0.377 AP50 in one training run.

4. **YOLOv8n is fast enough for the Jetson Orin Nano.** 18 ms / 55 FPS on an RTX 5090;
   expected ~25 ms on Orin Nano with TensorRT int8 (within the 30 ms budget).

5. **Epoch 1 mAP50:** v1 started at 0.037 (sim-only, hard classes); v2 started at 0.192
   (VisDrone prior). The warm start from VisDrone data means faster convergence when more
   data is added.
